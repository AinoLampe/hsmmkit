Segmentation and clustering metrics: `boundary_precision` / `boundary_recall` / `boundary_f1`, `hausdorff`, `covering`, `hamming_matched`, `confusion_matched`, `ari`, `nmi` (per-sequence, length-weighted aggregation helper), with a vendored Hungarian algorithm (`evaluation.metrics.matching.hungarian`, ADR-0020, no scipy at run time). `balanced_accuracy` now uses it and is no longer limited to 6 regimes. The `evaluation.metrics` gate row and `gates.PLANNED_METRICS` are removed.
