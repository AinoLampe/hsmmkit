Forward-filtering backward-sampling for both engines (`hsmmkit.engine.ffbs.ffbs_expanded` / `ffbs_explicit`): exact posterior regime paths, deterministic per key, with padding, supervision, topology and left-censoring. `decode(..., samples=R)` returns `path_samples` and `boundary_prob`; `hsmmkit decode --samples R` writes `path_samples.parquet` and a `boundary_prob` column in `posterior.parquet`.
