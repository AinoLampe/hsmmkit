VB: pruning and the VB-EM hybrid. Regimes whose expected occupancy is below `estimation.vb.prune_below` are reported in `FitResult.pruned_states` / `diagnostics["vb"]["occupancy"]` (and `PosteriorResult.pruned_states`); blocks without a conjugate update (covariate links, Student-t, irregular steps, fixed / bounded / tied / ordered parameters) keep point estimates updated by ECM on the expected counts, with a monotone partially collapsed ELBO and `diagnostics["vb"]["treatment"]`; `FitResult.std_errors` holds the posterior SDs of the conjugate blocks.
