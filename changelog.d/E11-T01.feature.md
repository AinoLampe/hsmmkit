`hsmmkit.testing.family_contract`: one importable pytest suite (`test_family_contract[<case>-<check>]`) that every emission family must pass; `EmissionFamily` gains the optional hooks `m_step`, `conjugate_prior`, `expected_log_prob`, `moments` (defaults in the `BaseEmission` mixin), `sufficient_stats` takes the covariate block `z`; `gaussian` and `student_t` provide `moments`.
