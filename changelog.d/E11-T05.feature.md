Markov-switching autoregression `msar` (`model.emission.ar: {p, form}`): regime-dependent coefficients with a weighted-least-squares M-step, Hamilton's mean-adjusted form for `p <= 2` on a new time-step memory state space (`memory_mode: steps`), own-lag covariates generated by `Preparer(..., own_lags=p)` (first `p` steps masked), AR stationarity diagnostic, recursive simulation.
