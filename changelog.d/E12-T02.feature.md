MAP estimation on a `SequenceBatch` (`fit(spec, batch, cfg, seed)`, `Project.fit/score/load_batch`): the objective sums the per-sequence log-likelihoods, the data-driven inits pool the sequences (windows never cross a boundary), `diagnostics["per_sequence"]` lists `(id, group, T, loglik)`. `model.initial_groups: per_group` (with `initial_state: free`) gives `initial.logits` one row per group; `simulate_batch(params, spec, lengths, key, groups=None)` draws batches. `initial_state: free` now creates its `initial.logits` parameter. The gate rows `data.sequences`, `data.source.sequence_column` and `model.initial_groups` are removed.
