Multivariate Student-t emission: a `student_t` configuration with `covariance: full` or the parameter names `mean` / `scale` / `chol` / `nu` selects a joint family with a diagonal or full scale matrix (`hsmmkit.model.emission.student_t.MultivariateStudentT`, `mvt_logpdf`); the univariate `m` / `s` / `nu` family is unchanged.
