The `Preparer` is domain-neutral: observation transforms are a registry (`identity`, `diff` in the core; `log_returns` moved to `regimekit.finance.transforms`), `missing_observations` gained `mask`, supports (`real`, `count`, `binary`, `categorical`) are validated, `standardize_observations` and `PrepStats.obs_mean` / `obs_std` were added, and time-less data is prepared by step index. The data-quality report is split: generic checks in `regimekit.data.quality`, finance checks in `regimekit.finance.quality`. `Grid.prices` is `Grid.values`; error paths say `data.preparation.missing_observations`.
