`estimation.method: vb`: mean-field variational Bayes (`VBEstimator`) for fully conjugate models on the expanded engine. Exact forward-backward `q(s)` on the expected log-parameters, Normal-Gamma / Normal-Wishart / Gamma / Beta / Dirichlet blocks with KL terms, Beta posteriors for the geometric / negative-binomial phase advance and Dirichlet jump rows, a monotone ELBO, posterior-mean `FitResult.params` and `diagnostics["vb"]`. The diagonal Gaussian family now has a Normal-Gamma conjugate prior; the Gaussian, Poisson, Bernoulli and Categorical families provide `expected_log_prob`. The `estimation.method=vb` feature gate is removed.
