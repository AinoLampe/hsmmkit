The CLI is an app factory (`regimekit.cli.app.build_app`): the core ships `regimekit-core` (no `fetch`), the finance app `regimekit` (`regimekit.finance.cli`) adds `fetch` and maps `ProviderError` to exit code 5 (`regimekit.cli.app.EXIT_CODES`, `register_exit_code`). `regimekit.project.Project` is domain-neutral (`load_data`); `regimekit.finance.project.FinanceProject` adds `fetch`, the strategy hook and the bundle extension; `regimekit.Project` stays the finance facade. `ProviderError` moved to `regimekit.finance.errors` and subclasses `DataError`. `SimResult.returns`, `ForecastPaths.returns` and `SimulationOutput.returns` are `values`. Both neutral-name allow-lists are gone.
