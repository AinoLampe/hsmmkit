`estimation.mcmc.sampler: gibbs`: blocked Gibbs sampling for fully conjugate models (FFBS state paths + conjugate block draws, chains `vmap`ped, optional `path_samples` group via `keep_paths_every`); post-hoc relabelling of MCMC draws towards the MAP pivot when nothing identifies the states (`estimation.relabel`, Hungarian matching, `relabelled_fraction` in the fit report); `permute_states` factored out of `post_sort_states`; `sample_params` on the conjugate classes.
