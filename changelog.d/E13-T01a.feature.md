`hsmmkit.engine.estep.expanded_estep`: the expanded-engine E-step in one forward pass and one fused backward scan, returning `ExpandedEStats` (per-sequence log-likelihoods, regime marginals, first-step posteriors per initial group, expected stay / advance / occupancy counts per duration-rate row and expected jump counts per jump-matrix row) without ever forming a `(T, S, S)` array; supports padded batches, supervision masks and tempering (`beta_temper`).
