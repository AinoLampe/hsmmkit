`estimation.method: em`: the `EMEstimator` (Baum-Welch on the expanded engine, a peer of MAP behind the new `estimator` registry kind; ADR-0018). Closed-form M-steps for the Gaussian (diagonal and full), Poisson, Bernoulli and Categorical emissions, the phase advance of geometric / negative-binomial durations, the jump rows (also under a topology) and the free initial distribution; padded batches, supervision, `fixed` / `bounded` parameters; multi-start with MAP's initializations; `FitResult(estimator="em")` with `loglik_trace`. `estimation.fit` dispatches on `estimation.method` (also in the walk-forward backtest). Components without a closed form are refused with a `ConfigError` naming E13-T02b. The GBM-only EM of E5-T06 is replaced (`em_fit` stays as a single-run wrapper); the `estimation.method=em` gate row is removed.
