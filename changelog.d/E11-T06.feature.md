Per-state finite mixture emission (`family: mixture`, `mixture: {components, base}`) of any registered base family: `N*K` pseudo-state parameters, `emission.weights`, within-state component ordering (`domain: increasing`), responsibility-based EM hooks, `K = 1` identical to the base. `FamilyCase.m_step_check` lets a family replace the generic M-step check. The `model.emission.mixture` gate is removed.
